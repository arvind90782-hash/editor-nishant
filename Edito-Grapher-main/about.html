About Editor Nishant

Professional freelance editing agency delivering premium video content for creators and brands worldwide.

Editor Nishant is a professional freelance editing agency led by Nishant Singh — a skilled Video Editor, Motion Graphics Designer, and Website Developer. We create high-quality videos, motion graphics, and web experiences for YouTube creators, brands, influencers, and businesses.

Our agency works with a small yet talented team of video editors, motion designers, and web specialists. Every high-level or detail-heavy project is personally handled by Nishant to ensure premium quality, clean visuals, modern storytelling, and polished online presence.
